A decision operating system: with agency as its premise, it uses the "Life Baseline" (人生横线) to guard a safety boundary that must not be broken, and uses "Low Friction" (低摩擦) to pick, inside that boundary, the path with the least resistance.

The core is to ask the "Life Baseline" first (is it meaningful, and can I bear the worst case), then "Low Friction" (is the path smooth, and is there structural resistance that cannot be removed). The two must be asked separately; neither can be dropped.

## About originality

"Low friction" is a general idea (path resistance, habit formation, execution friction); these dimensions of judgment have been widely discussed. The "Life Baseline" is the author's own formulation. This repository couples the two into one concrete, actionable, two-step judgment process. This way of coupling them, their ordering and the resulting structure were proposed and are maintained by the author, and this repository is its source version.
